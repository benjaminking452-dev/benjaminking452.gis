## About me

I'm a Geography graduate from the University of Bristol (BSc, 2025), and I make maps with GIS.

What I enjoy most is taking spatial data that's messy or hard to read (population grids, boundaries, land cover, survey points) and turning it into something people can understand at a glance. A good map answers a question before anyone has to ask it.

My degree is what got me hooked. Again and again, it showed me that *where* something happens shapes how it plays out: who's exposed to environmental risk, how places develop, where services are needed. GIS is how I've learned to test that rather than just describe it. I work in ArcGIS Pro and QGIS, and this portfolio grows as I take on new projects.
